Elastic vessels

Soft, stretchy arteries slow the wave down. The delay between ear and finger is long, around 100 to 140 ms.

Stiff or tense vessels

Hard or constricted arteries pass the wave quickly. The delay shrinks, below about 80 ms. Pick a state above and watch the gap close.

Why no cuff

The cuff squeezes to measure pressure once. Light clips watch every beat, so the reading updates continuously.

The numbers in each state are illustrative values for teaching. A real cuffless blood pressure estimate has to be calibrated for each person against a cuff reading, and this device is a learning prototype, not a medical instrument.
